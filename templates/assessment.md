## 2017 four-question subset

Teams framing a problem they were assigned use these four instead of the ten, then move on to solution discovery. See `statements/P-NNN.md`.

1. What business objective is this work intended to address?
2. How will you know if you've succeeded?
3. What problem will this solve for our customers?
4. What type of customer are we focused on?
