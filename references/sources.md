# Sources

Citation key for every `[S<n>]` in the references. S1–S122 are the numbered sources of the research this plugin was built from, in the same order, with titles and links as that research gives them. That list marks primary/secondary on most entries from S51 on and on none of S1–S50; the research itself treats S3, S8, S11, S12, S13, S18, S26, S33, S34, S35, S36, S39, S41 (the publisher's listing; the earlier link to a third-party copy of the book was removed), S43 and S44 as secondary (summaries, quote sites, third-party write-ups, a reader reply) and the SVPG articles and publisher book previews as primary. A few sources were reached only through previews or summaries. Where a reference says *synthesis*, it means this plugin's reading across the sources, not any one source's text.
